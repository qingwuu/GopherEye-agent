# Other Or Unresolved Conditions

Use this page for visible symptoms that are not sufficiently explained by the
canonical powdery mildew, downy mildew, or healthy pages. This page is a
differential holding area, not a substitute for disease-specific reviewed pages.

## Scope

```text
disease_id: others
common_names: other condition, unresolved condition, noncanonical differential
crop_scope: grape leaves
review_status: draft
```

## Included Differential Slots

These slots come from existing wiki treatment and differential placeholders:

```text
black rot or other necrotic leaf spot
anthracnose or other necrotic lesion disease
nutrient deficiency or physiological chlorosis
insect or mite damage
dust, spray residue, or soil residue
mechanical tearing or handling damage
lighting artifact, glare, shadow, or background confusion
```

Do not present these as reviewed disease pages until a dedicated page exists.
Use them as alternatives in [Diagnosis SOP](../../procedures/diagnosis_sop.md).

## Visual Patterns To Route Here

```text
generic yellowing without oil-spot pattern
brown spots or necrosis without enough lesion geometry
marginal scorch
dark specks or fruiting-body-like dots not covered by current disease pages
distortion, galls, insect-like damage, or mite-like damage
white-gray surface material that may be residue rather than colonies
severe old damage where the original disease sign is no longer visible
mixed powdery and downy signs in one case
```

## Required Handling

```text
1. Describe the visible abnormal sign before naming a candidate.
2. Compare against powdery mildew and downy mildew when relevant.
3. Compare against healthy or artifact explanations.
4. If evidence remains weak, use insufficient or provisional language.
5. Ask for the single most useful next image or observation.
6. Escalate to expert review when mixed signs or source gaps block confidence.
```

## Differentials

```text
powdery_mildew:
  link: ../powdery_mildew/index.md
  route there when superficial powdery colonies, webby mycelium, dusty conidia,
  or chasmothecia are visible

downy_mildew:
  link: ../downy_mildew/index.md
  route there when oil spots, angular vein-limited lesions, or underside cottony
  sporulation are visible

healthy:
  link: ../healthy/index.md
  route there when visible features are normal structures, nonblocking artifacts,
  or image limitations without disease-specific evidence
```

## Promotion Rule

Create a dedicated disease page when one of these conditions has enough reviewed
content to define identity, visual evidence thresholds, localization, common
differentials, image request behavior, and treatment boundaries.
