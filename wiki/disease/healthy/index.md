# Healthy Or Normal Variation

Use this page when a grape leaf image shows normal structures, nonblocking image
limitations, or artifacts without enough abnormal disease evidence.

## Identity

```text
disease_id: healthy
common_names: healthy, no visible disease evidence, normal grape leaf variation
pathogen_or_cause: none visible from image evidence
crop_scope: grape leaves
review_status: draft
```

## Normal Structures

These features are usually normal by themselves:

```text
lobed blade shape
serrated margins
prominent primary veins
secondary vein network
petiole attachment at the leaf base
surface color differences between adaxial and abaxial sides
minor shape asymmetry
raised veins on the lower surface
```

See [Grape Leaf Anatomy](../../reference/grape_leaf_anatomy.md).

## Normal Variation Or Artifacts

Do not diagnose disease from these alone:

```text
natural vein contrast
minor shadow from raised veins
leaf gloss on the upper surface
folding or curling caused by handling
small mechanical tears
lighting-induced pale areas
background soil, dust, or water spots
minor shadow, angle, or partial occlusion away from the symptomatic region
```

If the relevant leaf surface and suspected symptom remain inspectable, record
the limitation but continue the observation.

## Minimum Evidence For Healthy Label

```text
possible healthy:
  visible grape leaf structures with no clear abnormal signs

provisional healthy_or_normal_variation:
  image quality is sufficient to inspect the leaf, visible structures are normal
  or explainable by artifacts, and disease-specific evidence is absent

insufficient:
  image quality, framing, lighting, or side uncertainty prevents inspection of
  the suspected area
```

Avoid saying the plant is disease-free. Say only that the image does not show
visible disease evidence.

## Differentials To Check

```text
powdery_mildew:
  check for superficial white-gray powder, webby mycelium, or colonies
  link: ../powdery_mildew/index.md

downy_mildew:
  check for oil spots, angular vein-limited lesions, or underside cottony growth
  link: ../downy_mildew/index.md

other_or_unresolved:
  check for noncanonical spots, necrosis, deficiency-like chlorosis, insect or
  mite damage, spray residue, or mechanical damage
  link: ../others/index.md
```

## Image Requests

```text
clearer_same_view:
  when blur, overexposure, shadow, or occlusion blocks symptom inspection

opposite_surface_same_leaf:
  when a visible sign could be surface-specific disease evidence and one side is
  missing

wider_leaf_context:
  when only a close-up is available and distribution cannot be judged
```

Use [Image And Evidence SOP](../../procedures/image_and_evidence_sop.md) to
decide whether another image is needed.
