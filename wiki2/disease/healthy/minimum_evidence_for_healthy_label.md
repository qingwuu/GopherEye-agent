```text
possible healthy:
  visible grape leaf structures with no clear abnormal signs

provisional healthy_or_normal_variation:
  image quality is sufficient to inspect the leaf, visible structures are normal
  or explainable by artifacts, and disease-specific evidence is absent

insufficient:
  image quality, framing, lighting, or side uncertainty prevents inspection of
  the suspected area
```

Avoid saying the plant is disease-free. Say only that the image does not show
visible disease evidence.
